# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone contributing to this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome feedback and different perspectives.
- Focus on collaboration and constructive communication.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or threats.
- Trolling, insulting, or disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private or sensitive information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.
